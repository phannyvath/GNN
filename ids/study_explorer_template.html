Held-out evaluation
Primary method performance
Per-seed confusion counts
Precision: how many alerts were attacks. Recall: how many attacks were detected. F1 balances precision and recall; F2 gives recall more weight. AP summarizes precision–recall ranking (not trapezoidal PR-AUC). ROC-AUC summarizes attack/background ranking. FPR: the fraction of background targets incorrectly flagged. Undefined metrics appear as Unavailable. Scores are not calibrated probabilities. Models are simplified, inspired implementations.
